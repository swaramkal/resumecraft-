Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 2026

ResumeCraft operates as a client-side web application. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or store your personal resume details, uploaded files, cover letters, or job application records on external servers.

Local Browser Storage

Your work is stored exclusively in your browser's localStorage under isolated application keys so your progress persists across page refreshes. Clearing your browser site data removes this stored information unless you have exported a JSON backup.
